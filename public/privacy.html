All Ads Manager — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how All Ads Manager (“we”, “us”, or “the Service”) handles information when you use the Service.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, we may process account and profile details, customer support messages, campaign settings, advertising account identifiers, media and creative materials you upload, and information you submit through forms.

2. Connected advertising platforms

If you choose to connect an advertising platform, that platform may share account identifiers, campaign information, performance metrics, and other data permitted by the authorization you grant. The exact information depends on the platform, its APIs, and the permissions you approve.

Do not provide passwords, API secrets, access tokens, or other credentials in chat or support messages. Authorization credentials should be submitted only through the provider's official authorization flow and handled by the Service's server-side integration.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to provide account access, connect advertising accounts, prepare and manage campaigns, display reporting, provide customer support, protect the Service, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the Service, or disclosed where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer. When you direct the Service to interact with an advertising platform, relevant information is sent to that platform to carry out your request.

5. Storage and security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Access credentials and tokens, where supported, should be protected on the server and never exposed in public pages or source code.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary by data type and feature.

7. Your choices and rights

You may stop using a connected platform integration and revoke its access through the platform where that option is available. You may contact support through the Service to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use the relevant features.

9. International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy by publishing a revised version here and changing the effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support or contact channel provided inside All Ads Manager.
